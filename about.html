About SoyMutDB

SoyMutDB (Multi-Species Mutant Database) is a curated, open-access resource for chemically- and radiation-induced mutations, currently covering Glycine max (soybean) as the first/default species.

Consortium

The database is maintained by an international consortium of plant genetics laboratories and is released under a CC-BY 4.0 license.

Methodology

Variants are called from whole-genome sequencing of M3 mutant pools, aligned to the Wm82.a4.v1 reference, and annotated with SnpEff.
